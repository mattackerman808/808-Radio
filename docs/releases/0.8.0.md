# 808 Radio 0.8.0

**An FM + HD Radio receiver for Windows and macOS with RTL-SDR dongles, styled as a car stereo.**

![808 Radio](https://github.com/mattackerman808/808-Radio/raw/v0.8.0/docs/images/808radio.png)

## What it is

808 Radio turns a $30 RTL-SDR dongle into a proper radio: FM stereo with RDS, and **HD Radio** (NRSC-5) with album
art, multicast channels (HD1–HD8), weather and traffic maps, blended with the analog signal like a real HD receiver.
It finds the best gain for each station by itself and corrects the dongle's frequency error automatically.

The dongle can be on the computer or on a Raspberry Pi near the antenna running the standard `rtl_tcp` server, which
808 Radio finds on the network by itself.

## How it works

- **The receiver** is C#: the RTL-SDR's samples are filtered, demodulated to FM stereo with RDS, and the HD Radio
  sidebands go to [nrsc5](https://github.com/theori-io/nrsc5) for decoding. The analog and digital audio are blended
  the way a car receiver does it, so a weak HD station falls back to analog instead of dropping out.
- **The faceplate** is a car stereo: a dot-matrix display, HD program keys, presets, a tuning knob with detents.
  Press OPEN and it folds down to an instrument panel drawn at the display's refresh rate: spectrum and waterfall
  with click-to-tune, the FM multiplex, and live receiver statistics.
- **The weather map** is the station's own radar image (HD Radio's HERE image service) drawn over a street map of
  the same box from OpenStreetMap data, cached so it works without the internet.
- Windows builds use Windows Forms with Direct2D for the panel; macOS uses Avalonia. The receiver is the same code.

![The instrument panel behind the faceplate](https://github.com/mattackerman808/808-Radio/raw/v0.8.0/docs/images/808radio-open.png)

## What's new in 0.8.0

**Presets that know their HD program, and program switching the way a head unit does it.**

- **Presets remember the HD program.** Save a preset while listening to HD2 (or HD3 …) and recalling it brings back
  that program, not just the frequency. Each preset's little window reads like an LCD: the frequency, then a fixed
  **HD** legend with its own small digit, ghosts on an HD1 preset like any unlit segment and lit on one saved on HD2.
  The lit preset is the one whose frequency *and* program you're on. Presets saved by earlier versions stay HD1.

  ![Preset windows: 97.3 lit, 98.5 saved on HD2, and an empty one](https://github.com/mattackerman808/808-Radio/raw/v0.8.0/docs/images/808radio-presets.png)

- **Clearing a preset.** Hold a preset key to save, as before; keep holding about two seconds more and it's cleared:
  the display says `P2 CLEARED` and the window shows the ghost `88.8` again.
- **Switching HD programs is a short silence, not a spell of the analog.** HD CH used to drop you to the analog
  signal (HD1's audio) for a second or so while the new program buffered, and going back to HD1 meant six to eight
  seconds of analog while the HD-to-analog alignment was measured again. Now the old program fades out, the analog
  stays muted because it isn't the program you chose, and the new one comes on as soon as it's buffered: about half
  a second of silence. HD1's alignment is kept across the switch, so HD1 is back in the time it takes to buffer it.
  Nothing is re-peaked: the tuner gain only starts over on a new frequency.
- **Presets fixed.** A preset saved on HD2 always landed on HD1: the decoder reset the program when its session
  restarted after the retune, a moment after the preset had set it. And a preset for the station you're already on
  no longer retunes at all, so HD stays locked and the gain stays put when you jump between a station's HD1 and HD2
  presets. The HD program you were on when you closed 808 Radio is selected again when it starts.
- **The TUNE knob works with the wheel again.** Since the knobs moved to the right-hand corner in 0.7.0, scrolling
  over the TUNE knob changed the volume. The wheel changes the volume only over VOL now; over TUNE and over the
  display it tunes.

All of it is on both platforms.

## Download

- **Windows 10/11 (x64)**: `808Radio-v0.8.0-win-x64.zip`. Unzip anywhere and run `808Radio.exe`. Self-contained:
  no installer, no .NET to install. The dongle needs the WinUSB driver once (Zadig); see the README.
- **macOS (Apple Silicon)**: `808Radio-v0.8.0-mac-arm64.zip`. Unzip and open `808 Radio.app`. Signed and notarized,
  with the native libraries inside; a USB dongle needs no driver on macOS.

Upgrading keeps your settings and presets (`%APPDATA%\808Radio` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/808Radio`
on the Mac).

Full details in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/mattackerman808/808-Radio/blob/v0.8.0/CHANGELOG.md) and the
