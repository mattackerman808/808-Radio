# 808 Radio 0.9.0

**An FM + HD Radio receiver for Windows and macOS with RTL-SDR dongles, styled as a car stereo.**

![808 Radio](https://github.com/mattackerman808/808-Radio/raw/v0.9.0/docs/images/808radio.png)

## What it is

808 Radio turns a $30 RTL-SDR dongle into a proper radio: FM stereo with RDS, and **HD Radio** (NRSC-5) with album
art, multicast channels (HD1–HD8), weather and traffic maps, blended with the analog signal like a real HD receiver.
It finds the best gain for each station by itself and corrects the dongle's frequency error automatically.

The dongle can be on the computer, on a Raspberry Pi near the antenna running the standard `rtl_tcp` server, or on a
Mac in server mode; 808 Radio finds it on the network by itself.

## How it works

- **The receiver** is C#: the RTL-SDR's samples are filtered, demodulated to FM stereo with RDS, and the HD Radio
  sidebands go to [nrsc5](https://github.com/theori-io/nrsc5) for decoding. The analog and digital audio are blended
  the way a car receiver does it, so a weak HD station falls back to analog instead of dropping out.
- **The faceplate** is a car stereo: a dot-matrix display, HD program keys, presets, a tuning knob with detents.
  Press OPEN and it folds down to an instrument panel drawn at the display's refresh rate: spectrum and waterfall
  with click-to-tune, the FM multiplex, and live receiver statistics.
- **The weather map** is the station's own radar image (HD Radio's HERE image service) drawn over a street map of
  the same box from OpenStreetMap data, cached so it works without the internet.
- Windows builds use Windows Forms with Direct2D for the panel; macOS uses Avalonia. The receiver is the same code.

![The instrument panel behind the faceplate](https://github.com/mattackerman808/808-Radio/raw/v0.9.0/docs/images/808radio-open.png)

## What's new in 0.9.0

**Streaming: one dongle, any radio in the house.**

- **Server mode (macOS).** Right-click › *Server mode: share this dongle on the network*, and the Mac stops playing
  and serves its USB dongle over the network with the rtl_tcp protocol, the same way a Raspberry Pi does. It's
  advertised over Bonjour, so another 808 Radio lists it under *Source* by itself. One client at a time, the newest
  taking over; a slow or vanished client drops samples instead of wedging the dongle. The faceplate becomes a server
  panel showing who's being served, the frequency and the data rate, with a STOP SERVING key. (The Windows app can
  connect to a server but can't be one yet.)
- **808 Radio for Apple TV (TestFlight).** The faceplate on the big screen, on the same receiver, streaming from a
  network dongle: a Pi, or a Mac in server mode, both found under SETUP › SERVER. Dot-matrix display, HD programs,
  album art or a spectrum analyzer, six presets, all driven by the Siri Remote. It plays to the TV or to HomePods
  over AirPlay, and after a while idle a MilkDrop-style screen saver dances to the music. An Apple TV on Ethernet is
  best: the stream is about 24 Mbit/s.

## Download

- **Windows 10/11 (x64)**: `808Radio-v0.9.0-win-x64.zip`. Unzip anywhere and run `808Radio.exe`. Self-contained:
  no installer, no .NET to install. The dongle needs the WinUSB driver once (Zadig); see the README.
- **macOS (Apple Silicon)**: `808Radio-v0.9.0-mac-arm64.zip`. Unzip and open `808 Radio.app`. Signed and notarized,
  with the native libraries inside; a USB dongle needs no driver on macOS.

Upgrading keeps your settings and presets (`%APPDATA%\808Radio` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/808Radio`
on the Mac).
